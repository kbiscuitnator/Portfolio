- Page background stays white.

## Look

The link is a one-page academic research poster (a single wide slide). In plain words:

- **Background:** plain white, clean and bright.
- **Colors:** two accent colors only. A deep plum / wine purple for the main title, and a warm burnt orange for section labels and box outlines. Body text is black.
- **Fonts:** simple, bold sans-serif headings; small, plain sans-serif body text. No decorative fonts.
- **Layout:** a grid of columns, with each topic sitting in its own box that has a thin orange outline and rounded corners. Each box has a solid orange label tab across the top with white text (for example "Introduction", "Methods", "Results").
- **Visuals:** content is carried by charts, maps, small illustrations of patients, tables and screenshots, each with a short numbered caption underneath.
- **Header:** large title across the top left, author names and affiliations underneath in smaller text, and partner logos on the right.
- **Extras:** QR codes in the bottom corner for references and code.
- **Overall feel:** academic and organized. It reads like a scientific poster: structured, information-dense and easy to scan section by section.

For the website, the parts to borrow are the white background, the plum + orange accent pair, the rounded outlined "card" boxes with a colored label, and charts with captions. The site should be less crowded than the poster, with more space between sections.
